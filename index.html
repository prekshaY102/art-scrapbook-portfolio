why me?

Every photo holds a memory. I turn it into art.

I'm Preksha — an artist who loves turning memories, ideas, and feelings into something you can hold. I work with graphite, colour, acrylic, clay, lippan, mandala, and whatever lets me create with my hands. For me, art is about making something beautiful and personal.

Every piece begins with a photo, a memory, or a feeling — then I rebuild it by hand, one line, colour, and little detail at a time.
