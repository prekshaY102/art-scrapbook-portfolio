why me?

Every photo holds a memory. I turn it into art.

I'm Preksha — an artist who loves turning memories, ideas, and feelings into something you can hold. I work with graphite pencils, charcoal pencils, colours, clay and whatever lets me create with my hands. For me, art is about making something beautiful and personal.

Every piece begins with a photo, a memory, or a feeling — then I rebuild it by hand, one line, colour, and little detail at a time.

frequently asked questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

How long does it take to create a custom portrait?

It typically takes 2-3 weeks from the time you submit your photo and preferences.

What if I'm not happy with the final product?

I'm committed to your satisfaction. If you're not happy, we can discuss revisions or adjustments.

Can a portrait be made as an anniversary or birthday gift?

Yes. Share a clear photo and the story behind it, and I'll create a personalised portrait by hand.

What kind of photo works best?

A clear, well-lit photo where the face or subject is easy to see. Send the highest quality you have.

Are clay keepsakes a good gift for birthdays and anniversaries?

Yes. They're small, personal and easy to carry or display, which makes them a thoughtful gift for couples, friends and family.
